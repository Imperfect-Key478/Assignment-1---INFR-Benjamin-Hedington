About Me

I am 19 years old, and have been interested in technology and programming for over 10 years, starting with simple block based coding before moving to Python and then C# when I was in high school. I've always been interested in how computers work, how to make them work and how to protect them from the internet. Ontario Tech University was my first choice for post secondary as I used to do summer camps here every year for most of my summer holidays, specifically the coding, minecraft and lego robotics camps. Networking and IT security was my first choice of program as I wanted to have a interesting and stable career in a uncertain, AI forward world currently. I personally have been running a Kali Linux dual boot since the beginning of the semester for computer security and penetration testing, and have been learning about the different methods of testing, which has been interesting.
